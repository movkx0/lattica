Evidence and limits

Full measurements, geometry, commands, source identity, and receipt hashes · Previous profile

  • No complete fresh N3 or N4 workflow was timed. Imported native proofs are revalidated; these timings exclude native transaction proof generation.
  • The graph comparison covers the same eight GBound native leaves at offsets 4..11 in transaction 0 of the deterministic N4 archive.
  • The parent screen covers eight singleton native groups, two four-child parents, and a two-child root; one timing per revision.
  • The SHAKE screen covers the first 16 segments of request 1, a partial 109-segment request. Its reported timings measure GPU proof execution only.
  • First-use and warm timings are reported separately; the exact source of any first-use initialization difference has not been isolated.
  • The graph first sample increased from 19.317 to 20.216 seconds. SHAKE has one warm sample in this revision versus two in the baseline; its roughly 3% warm difference is small enough to warrant repeated measurements before treating it as a stable gain.
  • A split adds a table and may increase parent verification work; the existing table and quotient limits remain fail-closed.
